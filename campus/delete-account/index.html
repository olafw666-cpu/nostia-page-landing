Terms of Use · Privacy Policy · Support · Delete your account

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Nostia Campus (Nostia LLC) and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Account and legal, then Delete account.
  3. Enter your password, tap Delete account, and confirm.

The deletion happens at once and you are signed out. It cannot be undone.

Or ask us by email

If you can't use the app, email nostiaexecutive@nostia.io from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Nostia Campus account". We may ask you to confirm by replying. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, password and passkeys
  • Your school and club memberships
  • Messages you posted in club chats, and your blocks
  • Your RSVPs, event check-ins and survey answers
  • The positions recorded during your walks
  • Your notifications and your sign-in sessions

What is kept, and for how long

  • Anonymous totals. Walks you completed and events you attended remain in schools' totals as numbers that no longer identify you, with no positions.
  • Content you made for a club, such as a walking trip you authored, stays with the club with your name removed.
  • Reports. A report you made, or one made about a message of yours, is kept as a moderation record without your name, including up to 500 characters of the reported message, while it is reviewed and for one year after it is resolved.
  • Backups are overwritten within 14 days.
  • Security records (sign-in events with time and IP address) are deleted after 90 days; ask us and we will delete them sooner.

If you lead a club or own a school

A club you lead passes to its longest-serving officer; if it has none, it is paused until your school names a new leader, and the school is told. If you are the owner of a school's account, transfer it to someone else in the web console first; the app tells you if this applies.
