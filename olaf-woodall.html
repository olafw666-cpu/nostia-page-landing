Nostia — AI orientation for universities.

Nostia-Orgs is the one app a university connects through: every club, every meeting and verified attendance at all of them, with chat and announcements built in — plus AI campus tours and the orientation events an institution hosts.

Where it stands

The student app is live on the iOS App Store. Nostia-Orgs, the platform a university subscribes to, launches for the Fall 2027 semester.

Infrastructure that stopped losing data

A native Swift and SwiftUI app on a Node.js, Express and SQLite backend. I moved that backend off a managed platform after its ephemeral containers kept wiping the database on every deploy; it now runs on a self-managed Linux server with Nginx, PM2, TLS and automated daily backups.

Payments on Stripe Connect v2

Direct charges and Apple Pay through Stripe Connect v2, including a full migration to Connect v2's new account model.

The company

A five-person team, which I run as CEO. I formed Nostia LLC in New Hampshire and co-authored and stress-tested the company charter.

My working style is spec-driven: major features and hardware systems get formal engineering specifications before and during implementation.

Shipped

Proximity kill-switch (“Drone Ping”)

  • ESP32-C3 based proximity kill-switch using ESP-NOW peer-to-peer radio — hardware confirmed working.
  • Diagnosed and fixed a MOSFET drive problem by selecting a logic-level part (IRLZ44N) that fully saturates at a 3.3V gate.
  • Corrected the power topology so the switch interrupts motor power only, not the main battery rail.
  • Documented in a set of formal PDFs; code is public on GitHub.
In progress

Impact-recovery drone

  • A 5-inch, 6S dual-battery quadcopter engineered to survive sub-40 mph impacts and recover flight.
  • Elastic TPU motor mounts absorb impact energy; recovery combines a contact bumper with differential pre-impact motor power bias.
  • An offline vision-language model handles post-flight analysis — deliberately kept out of the real-time control loop after evaluating latency constraints.
